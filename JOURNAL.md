# October 7, 2026: parts hunt!
<!-- fabricate:entry 184 -->

today i started figuring out what parts i actually wanna use for kaze.
i decided on a **65% wireless** design with **bluetooth** and **ZMK**, and after looking through a bunch of stuff i finally settled on the **HMX Xinhai switches**, **MDA crystal keycaps**, **durock stabilisers**, **nRF52840**, **SK6812-MINI-E leds** and **1N4148 diodes**.
i'll have to keep the whole thing under $125, so now that the main parts are decided i can finally start working on the actual design.
