# October 7, 2026: parts hunt!
<!-- fabricate:entry 184 -->

today i started figuring out what parts i actually wanna use for kaze.

i decided on a **65% wireless** design with **bluetooth** and **ZMK**, and after looking through a bunch of stuff i finally settled on the **HMX Taro switches**, **MDA crystal keycaps**, **durock stabilisers**, **nRF52840**, **SK6812-MINI-E leds**, **1N4148 diodes**, and **gateron hotswap sockets**.

i'm also going with a **3.7V 1100mAh LiPo**, a **C&K power switch**.

for the case, i've decided to 3d print a **thin case**. it'll use a **screwless tray-mount design**, with the pcb sitting below it.

i'll have to keep the whole thing under **$125**, so now that the main parts are decided i can finally start working on the actual design.

**next up is the schematic!**
